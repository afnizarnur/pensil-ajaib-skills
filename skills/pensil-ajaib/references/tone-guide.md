# Tone Guide

For full tone details, read the tribe files in `Core Guidelines/`.

## Murid Tribe

**Tone meters:**
- Playful: 7/10 (PRIMARY)
- Encouraging: 6/10 (SECONDARY)
- Directive: 4/10
- Straightforward: 3/10

**Pronoun**: Always `kamu` — never `Anda`
**Style**: Light, motivating, game-like. Emphasize progress, rewards, peer validation.
**Motivators**: Grades, task completion, exam prep, social proof (teman-temanmu juga...)

## GTK Tribe

4 user types, each with distinct tone:

| User | Tone | Formality | Key trait |
|------|------|-----------|-----------|
| Guru | Warm, appreciative | Semi-formal | Softest tone; collegial |
| Pengawas Sekolah | Balanced, regulatory | Formal | Compliance + support |
| Kepala Sekolah | Executive, performance-aware | Formal | Data-driven, strategic |
| Dinas Pendidikan | Firm, data-driven | Highly formal | Regulatory authority |

**Pronoun**: `Anda` (all GTK users)
**Style**: Professional, respectful, never condescending. Emphasize impact and outcomes.

## SPM Tribe

3 Ruang (spaces):
- **Ruang Sekolah** (Kepala Sekolah, Bendahara): Operational, supportive, product-aware
- **Ruang Pemerintah** (Dinas, Kementerian): Administrative, regulatory
- **Ruang Mitra**: Professional; may use parenthetical English terms

**Pronoun**: `Anda`
**Style**: Administrative, clear, process-oriented. No motivational/emotional language.

## Universal Tone Rules (All Tribes)

- Never blame users for errors
- Benefits-driven, not feature-driven (what it does FOR them, not what it IS)
- Empathetic and supportive at all times
- Action-oriented: tell users what to do next
- Confident but not arrogant
