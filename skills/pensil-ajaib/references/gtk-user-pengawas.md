# Ruang GTK Copy Guideline — Pengawas Sekolah (STRICT)
_Target User-based Guideline - Always Active_
_Last updated: 16 Feb 2026_

This guide is for generating UX content for Pengawas Sekolah using Ruang GTK. Pengawas are regulatory interpreters and quality assurers — communication must be precise, role-aligned, literal, and professionally equal.

## **Content Priorities**
Pengawas focus on **clarity of role, regulatory updates, actionable CTA, and impact on supervised schools**.

### **Primary Motivators**
* Clear alignment with regulation
* Understanding the core message quickly
* Impact on output of schools they’re assigned on
* Assurance they are acting within rules
* Maintaining authority and credibility

### **Framing Examples**
* ✅ “Berikut pembaruan regulasi yang perlu kita pahami.”
* ✅ “Langkah ini mendukung peran kita dalam mendampingi sekolah.”
* ✅ “Tombol berikut dapat digunakan untuk…”

## **Audience Insights**

### **Reading Patterns**
* [PGW-AUD-GTK-01] **Skim → deep-read important parts:**  
   Skims first to locate key information, then reads all relevant sections carefully.
  * **Solution:** Make key information easy to locate
  * **Solution:** Highlight core points

* [PGW-AUD-GTK-02] **Literal word-by-word interpretation:**  
   Interprets wording strictly and literally.
  * **Solution:** Avoid ambiguous language
  * **Solution:** Use precise terminology

* [PGW-AUD-GTK-03] **Core-message seeker:**  
   Wants the essence immediately.
  * **Solution:** Lead with the main point
  * **Solution:** Avoid long introduction

* [PGW-AUD-GTK-04] **Sensitive to authoritarian signals:**  
   Exclamation mark (\!) perceived as harsh command.
  * **Solution:** Avoid exclamation marks
  * **Solution:** Avoid commanding tone

### **Information Hierarchy**
* [PGW-AUD-GTK-05] **What matters most:**
  * Main CTA (button, hyperlink)
  * Information impacting supervised school performance
  * Regulatory updates
  * **Solution:** Surface CTA clearly
  * **Solution:** Clarify impact on schools
  * **Solution:** Highlight regulatory updates

### **Visual & Format Preferences**
* [PGW-AUD-GTK-06] **Minimal visual dependency:**  
   Does not focus on decorative visuals; prioritizes message clarity.
  * **Solution:** Avoid decorative illustrations
  * **Solution:** Use structure over visual complexity

* [PGW-AUD-GTK-07] **Highlighting preference:**  
   Responds to bold or background highlight for key information.
  * **Solution:** Use bold for critical words
  * **Solution:** Use subtle background highlights when needed

### **Tone & Positioning**
* [PGW-AUD-GTK-08] **Equal professional positioning:**  
   Must feel positioned as knowledgeable and equal to Kemendikbud, not merely executor.
  * **Solution:** Use “kita” framing
  * **Solution:** Avoid hierarchical commands

* [PGW-AUD-GTK-09] **No negative implication:**  
   Do not imply incompetence or doubt capability.
  * **Solution:** Avoid skeptical or corrective tone

* [PGW-AUD-GTK-10] **Direct but not verbose:**  
   No motivational filler or flowery language.
  * **Solution:** Be concise
  * **Solution:** Remove unnecessary emotional framing

* [PGW-AUD-GTK-11] **Regulation-aligned vocabulary:**  
   Must use terminology consistent with regulation (e.g., “mendampingi,” not “memantau/menilai”).
  * **Solution:** Mirror regulatory wording precisely

* [PGW-AUD-GTK-12] **Time sensitivity caution:**  
   Avoid specific deadlines unless formally mandated.
  * **Solution:** Only mention time if required by regulation

### **Emotional & Psychological Context**
Pengawas need to feel:
* Professionally respected
* Included in decision context
* Assured they are not violating regulation
* Confident they are informed before Kepala Sekolah

Avoid:
* Authoritarian commands
* Overly motivational language
* Doubt-inducing phrasing
* Over-specific restrictions without regulatory basis

### **Trust & Influence**
* [PGW-AUD-GTK-13] **Trusted sources:**
  * Dinas
  * Senior Pengawas
  * **Solution:** Reference authority where relevant
  * **Solution:** Maintain professional credibility tone

### **Media & Channels**
* [PGW-AUD-GTK-14] **Preferred channels:**
  * WA Group
  * Official circular letters
  * Official association groups
  * **Solution:** Ensure message works when shared via WA
  * **Solution:** Keep summary compact and clear

### **Troubleshooting Behavior**
* [PGW-AUD-GTK-15] **Peer consultation first:**
  * Ask fellow Pengawas
  * Search independently (e.g., Google)
  * **Solution:** Provide clear, searchable phrasing
  * **Solution:** Make help/documentation easy to access

## **Structural Writing Rules**
For regulatory updates:
* Lead with update
* Clarify implication for supervisory role
* Present action
* Avoid emotional framing

For instructions:
* Use bullet points
* Avoid over-detailed micromanagement
* Leave space for contextual adaptation

For emphasis:
* Bold key regulatory terms
* Avoid exclamation marks
* Avoid ALL CAPS except where formally necessary

## **Tone Summary**
**Be:**
* Precise
* Equal
* Regulation-aligned
* Direct
* Respectful
* Action-oriented

**Avoid:**
* Exclamation marks
* Authoritarian tone
* Flowery language
* Negative implications
* Non-regulatory terminology
