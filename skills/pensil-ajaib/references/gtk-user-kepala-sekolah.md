# Ruang GTK Copy Guideline — Kepala Sekolah (STRICT)
_Target User-based Guideline - Always Active_
_Last updated: 16 Feb 2026_

This guide is for generating UX content for Kepala Sekolah using Ruang GTK. Kepala Sekolah are institutional decision-makers — communication must be concise, strategic, solution-oriented, and credibility-driven.

## **Content Priorities**
Kepala Sekolah focus on **school performance, urgency, competitiveness, compliance, and funding**.

### **Primary Motivators**
* School performance improvement
* Solving operational or performance problems
* Compliance with Dinas/regulation
* Funding and resource allocation
* Competitive positioning vs. other schools
* Maintaining credibility when questioned

### **Framing Examples**
* ✅ “Berikut ringkasan kondisi sekolah Anda dan langkah prioritas yang dapat dilakukan.”
* ✅ “Sekolah lain sudah menerapkan praktik ini untuk meningkatkan capaian.”
* ✅ “Panduan ini membantu Anda mengambil keputusan cepat dalam 15 menit.”

## **Audience Insights**

### **Reading Patterns**
* [KPS-AUD-GTK-01] **High-speed skimming:**  
   Reads 1–2 opening sentences or highlighted keywords.
  * **Solution:** Front-load the most urgent or important information
  * **Solution:** Use strong headlines and bold keywords

* [KPS-AUD-GTK-02] **Limited time allocation:**  
   Only willing to spend \~15–20 minutes reading (max 1 A4 page).
  * **Solution:** Keep content to one focused action per page
  * **Solution:** Provide executive summary at the top

* [KPS-AUD-GTK-03] **Practical-first mindset:**  
   Looks for practical solutions before theory.
  * **Solution:** Start with actionable steps
  * **Solution:** Move theoretical explanation after action

* [KPS-AUD-GTK-04] **Sequential reading for unfamiliar topics:**  
   If the topic is new, reads from start to finish.
  * **Solution:** Provide logical flow
  * **Solution:** Avoid jumping between concepts

* [KPS-AUD-GTK-05] **Senior resistance to novelty:**  
   The more senior the Kepsek, the lower the tendency to explore new systems.
  * **Solution:** Reduce cognitive load
  * **Solution:** Emphasize ease and clarity
  * **Solution:** Avoid complex digital jargon

### **Information Hierarchy**
* [KPS-AUD-GTK-06] **What attracts attention:**
  * High urgency (e.g., poor performance)
  * Comparison with other schools
  * Regulatory instruction from Dinas
  * Funding-related information
  * **Solution:** Place urgent or competitive hooks at the beginning
  * **Solution:** Highlight funding and regulatory relevance clearly

### **Visual & Format Preferences**
* [KPS-AUD-GTK-07] **High-level comprehension tools:**
  * Graphs, tables, color indicators
  * Red \= poor, Yellow \= moderate, Green \= good (strong association)
  * **Solution:** Use simple data visualization
  * **Solution:** Keep visuals minimal but functional
  * **Solution:** Avoid decorative illustrations

* [KPS-AUD-GTK-08] **Trust signals via visuals:**  
   Logos and institutional markers increase credibility.
  * **Solution:** Include official branding when possible

* [KPS-AUD-GTK-09] **Highlighting technique:**  
   Responds to bold, CAPSLOCK, centered key statements.
  * **Solution:** Highlight only truly critical information

### **Tone & Framing**
* [KPS-AUD-GTK-10] **Confidence-building principle:**  
   Kepsek must feel confident answering questions from teachers or stakeholders.
  * **Solution:** Provide clear summary points they can reuse verbally
  * **Solution:** Avoid ambiguous explanations

* [KPS-AUD-GTK-11] **Problem-focused framing:**  
   Action is triggered when framed around school problems.
  * **Solution:** Start with the issue
  * **Solution:** Immediately follow with solution

* [KPS-AUD-GTK-12] **Competitive nudge:**  
   Highly responsive to social proof.
  * **Solution:** Mention adoption by other schools
  * **Solution:** Use comparative data when relevant

* [KPS-AUD-GTK-13] **Two-way communication style:**  
   Engagement increases with reflective or question-based framing.
  * **Solution:** Use light rhetorical questions
  * **Solution:** Alternate between first and third person perspective

### **Trust & Influence**
* [KPS-AUD-GTK-14] **Trusted voices:**
  * Senior Kepala Sekolah (mentor/coach figure)
  * Dinas / Pemerintah daerah
  * **Solution:** Reference authority when relevant
  * **Note:** Dinas increases credibility but does not always guarantee behavior change

### **Media & Channels**
* [KPS-AUD-GTK-15] **Preferred channels:**
  * Personal chat / phone
  * YouTube
  * Official webinars
  * Official circular letters
  * **Solution:** Tone should work both in written format and verbal summary
  * **Solution:** Structure content that can be easily explained verbally

### **Troubleshooting Behavior**
* [KPS-AUD-GTK-16] **Problem resolution style:**
  * Ask peers interactively
  * Search for info/help button in platform
  * **Solution:** Make help features visible
  * **Solution:** Label support clearly and directly

## **Structural Writing Rules**
For long information:
* Provide short narrative summary at the top
* One primary action per page
* Order information from most important to least
* Keep critical information visible in one view

For guides:
* Use step-by-step practical format
* Place instructions close to the action button
* Use clear verbs

## **Tone Summary**
**Be:**
* Executive
* Practical
* Urgency-aware
* Credible
* Competitive-aware
* Concise

**Avoid:**
* Overly theoretical explanation upfront
* Long dense paragraphs
* Excessive illustration
* Weak headlines
* Ambiguous summaries
