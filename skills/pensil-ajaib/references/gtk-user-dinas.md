# Ruang GTK Copy Guideline — Dinas Pendidikan (STRICT)
_Target User-based Guideline - Always Active_
_Last updated: 16 Feb 2026_

This guide is for generating UX content for **Dinas Pendidikan (Kab/Kota/Provinsi)** using Ruang GTK. Dinas operate at a governance and coordination level — communication must be regulatory-aligned, data-backed, authoritative, and immediately actionable within their formal authority.

## **Content Priorities**
Dinas focus on **regulatory compliance, cross-ministry alignment, data accuracy, authority boundaries, and risk mitigation**.

### **Primary Motivators**
* Alignment with Kemendagri & Kemendikbud regulation
* Clear authority boundaries (sesuai kewenangan)
* Data-driven oversight
* Avoiding public protest or structural pressure
* Ensuring downstream implementation (schools & kepala sekolah)

### **Framing Examples**
* ✅ “Sesuai regulasi Kemendagri No. XX/202X, Dinas dapat melakukan…”
* ✅ “Data menunjukkan 27% sekolah di wilayah Anda belum…”
* ✅ “Langkah ini membantu mencegah potensi kendala implementasi di sekolah.”

## **Audience Insights**

### **Reading Patterns**
* [DNS-AUD-GTK-01] **Authority-based scanning:**  
   Skims directly to sections relevant to their area of authority.
  * **Solution:** Segment content clearly by role/authority
  * **Solution:** Use bold headers indicating scope of action

* [DNS-AUD-GTK-02] **Detail needed at a glance:**  
   Requires detailed information but must be quickly digestible.
  * **Solution:** Use structured tables
  * **Solution:** Present summaries before detailed breakdown

* [DNS-AUD-GTK-03] **Low tolerance for scrolling:**
  * **Solution:** Avoid long vertical flows
  * **Solution:** Keep key information visible in one screen view

* [DNS-AUD-GTK-04] **Cross-check behavior:**  
   Frequently verifies alignment across K/L (Kemendagri, Kemendikbud, etc.).
  * **Solution:** Cite regulatory references clearly
  * **Solution:** Use terminology consistent with official regulation

* [DNS-AUD-GTK-05] **Prefers technical tutorials:**
  * **Solution:** Provide practical, step-by-step technical guidance
  * **Solution:** Avoid abstract conceptual framing

### **Information Hierarchy**
* [DNS-AUD-GTK-06] **What matters most:**
  * Items within their authority to act on
  * Latest central regulations/instructions
  * Regional/school comparative data
  * Numeric performance indicators
  * **Solution:** Lead with numbers, not narrative
  * **Solution:** Clarify “Dapat ditindaklanjuti oleh Dinas” explicitly

### **Visual & Format Preferences**
* [DNS-AUD-GTK-07] **Table-oriented comprehension:**  
   Tables preferred over graphs (graphs lack sufficient detail).
  * **Solution:** Use structured tables for breakdowns
  * **Solution:** Avoid decorative visualizations

* [DNS-AUD-GTK-08] **Credibility markers:**  
   Institutional logos and formal formatting increase trust.
  * **Solution:** Include official references/logos when appropriate

* [DNS-AUD-GTK-09] **Minimal illustration tolerance:**  
   Illustrations are irrelevant unless adding credibility or meaning.
  * **Solution:** Only include visuals that strengthen authority

* [DNS-AUD-GTK-10] **Keyword highlighting:**
  * **Solution:** Bold regulatory references, deadlines, authority terms

### **Tone & Positioning**
* [DNS-AUD-GTK-11] **Firm but non-dictating authority:**  
   Tone should be decisive, confident, and direct — but not patronizing.
  * **Solution:** Use instructive verbs
  * **Solution:** Avoid emotional language
  * **Solution:** Avoid overly persuasive marketing tone

* [DNS-AUD-GTK-12] **Regulatory alignment language:**  
   Must align terminology with Kemendagri (even if jargon-heavy).
  * **Solution:** Mirror regulatory wording exactly
  * **Solution:** Avoid paraphrasing legal terminology loosely

* [DNS-AUD-GTK-13] **Urgency framing:**  
   Action is triggered when there is structural or reputational risk.
  * **Solution:** Frame potential downstream consequences (e.g., public complaints, audit issues)
  * **Solution:** Highlight implementation gaps

* [DNS-AUD-GTK-14] **Flexibility within central mandate:**  
   Appreciate space to adapt central directives to regional context.
  * **Solution:** Provide room for contextual adjustment
  * **Solution:** Avoid rigid step enforcement unless mandated

### **Trust & Influence**
* [DNS-AUD-GTK-15] **Trusted institutions:**
  * Kemendagri
  * Pemerintah Daerah
  * **Solution:** Reference alignment between Kemendikbud & Kemendagri
  * **Solution:** Reinforce cross-ministry coherence to increase buy-in

### **Media & Channels**
* [DNS-AUD-GTK-16] **Preferred channels:**
  * Official government letters
  * Formal regulations
  * Official webinars
  * **Solution:** Ensure tone works in formal written documentation
  * **Solution:** Avoid casual conversational phrasing

### **Troubleshooting Behavior**
* [DNS-AUD-GTK-17] **Escalation-based problem solving:**  
   Contacts PIC from pusat (especially field-connected representatives).
  * **Solution:** Clearly state responsible contact point
  * **Solution:** Provide clear accountability structure

## **Structural Writing Rules**
For regulatory updates:
* Start with regulatory reference
* Follow with what falls under Dinas authority
* Present data (if available)
* Conclude with required/possible action

For dashboards or data summaries:
* Use tables over charts
* Highlight key figures
* Clarify comparative position (region vs national benchmark)

## **Emotional & Psychological Context**
Dinas need to feel:
* **Aligned** with central government
* **Credible** in front of superiors and local stakeholders
* **Protected** from downstream backlash
* **Relieved** that the system simplifies coordination

Avoid:
* Emotional persuasion
* Casual tone
* Excessive narrative explanation
* Unclear authority boundaries

## **Tone Summary**
**Be:**
* Regulatory
* Structured
* Data-driven
* Firm
* Cross-ministry aligned
* Operationally clear

**Avoid:**
* Marketing-style copy
* Abstract motivational framing
* Decorative visuals
* Loose terminology
* Ambiguous accountability
