# Ruang GTK Copy Guideline — GURU (STRICT)
_Target User-based Guideline - Always Active_
_Last updated: 16 Feb 2026_

This guide is for generating UX content for Guru using Ruang GTK. Guru are frontline implementers — communication must be literal, practical, appreciative, and directly relevant to their professional reality.

## **Content Priorities**
Guru focus on **clear action, direct benefits, student outcomes, rights, and career progression**.

### **Primary Motivators**
* Improving student learning outcomes
* Career advancement (sertifikasi, pelatihan, kenaikan jabatan)
* Recognition of achievement
* Fulfillment of rights (honor, tunjangan, dll)
* Clear instruction on what to do next

### **Framing Examples**
* ✅ “Ini membantu meningkatkan hasil belajar murid Anda.”
* ✅ “Lengkapi data ini untuk memastikan hak Anda terpenuhi.”
* ✅ “Mari selesaikan langkah berikut dalam 3 menit.”

## **Audience Insights**

### **Reading Patterns**
* [GRU-AUD-GTK-01] **Extreme skimming behavior:**  
   Reads title carefully, but often only 1 paragraph (max 2 scrolls), then skims.
  * **Solution:** Put the core message in the title
  * **Solution:** Keep paragraphs short
  * **Solution:** Surface the action immediately

* [GRU-AUD-GTK-02] **Action-focused attention:**  
   Focuses on what needs to be clicked, filled, or watched.
  * **Solution:** Use strong action verbs
  * **Solution:** Make primary action visually clear

* [GRU-AUD-GTK-03] **Literal interpretation:**  
   Prefers wording that minimizes misinterpretation.
  * **Solution:** Avoid abstract phrasing
  * **Solution:** Use concrete, step-based instructions

* [GRU-AUD-GTK-04] **Selective attention in structured content:**  
   In long questions (e.g., 1–40), focus only on numbers.
  * **Solution:** Use numbering carefully
  * **Solution:** Avoid overwhelming structure

### **Information Hierarchy**
* [GRU-AUD-GTK-05] **What matters most:**
  * Clear benefit & relevance to teachers
  * Student learning outcomes
  * Career progression
  * Rights & compensation
  * Achievement results
  * **Solution:** State benefit explicitly and early
  * **Solution:** Tie actions to professional growth or student impact

### **Visual & Format Preferences**
* [GRU-AUD-GTK-06] **Needs visual breaks:**  
   Dense text discourages engagement.
  * **Solution:** Use bullet points
  * **Solution:** Break long paragraphs
  * **Solution:** Use background highlights for important info

* [GRU-AUD-GTK-07] **Text-supported visuals:**  
   Prefers visual formats accompanied by explanatory text (e.g., comic-style).
  * **Solution:** Combine visual cue + literal explanation

### **Tone & Positioning**
* [GRU-AUD-GTK-08] **Appreciative tone requirement:**  
   Do not blame or imply lack of effort.
  * **Solution:** Use appreciative language
  * **Solution:** Avoid phrases implying incompetence

* [GRU-AUD-GTK-09] **Empowerment over command:**  
   Respond better to “saran,” “panduan,” “anjuran” than rigid command.
  * **Solution:** Avoid overly commanding tone
  * **Solution:** Use inviting phrasing (“Mari…”)

* [GRU-AUD-GTK-10] **Personalization increases engagement:**
  * **Solution:** Personalize by jenjang, mata pelajaran, or name where possible

### **Trust & Influence**
* [GRU-AUD-GTK-11] **Trusted voices:**
  * Fellow teachers (especially from same region)
  * Kepala Sekolah
  * **Solution:** Use peer-based framing where relevant
  * **Solution:** Maintain credible, institutional clarity

### **Media & Channels**
* [GRU-AUD-GTK-12] **Preferred channels:**
  * WA Group (most dominant)
  * FB Group
  * Official webinars
  * Official circular letters (e.g., SIMPKB)
  * **Solution:** Write in a format that works when screenshotted and shared in WA
  * **Solution:** Keep summaries concise

### **Troubleshooting Behavior**
* [GRU-AUD-GTK-13] **Peer-first problem solving:**
  * Ask in interactive forum to fellow teachers
  * Look for help/information button in platform
  * **Solution:** Make help button visible
  * **Solution:** Provide concise FAQs

## **Structural Writing Rules**
For announcements:
* Core benefit in headline
* Short paragraph summary
* One main action per page
* Use bullet points if more than one item

For instructions:
* Use numbering for sequential steps
* Keep each step literal and concise
* Place action button close to instruction

For emphasis:
* Bold important words
* Use background color highlight for critical information

## **Emotional & Psychological Context**
Guru need to feel:
* Appreciated
* Not blamed
* Supported
* Clear about next step
* Professionally recognized

Avoid:
* Implying they are lacking
* Overly technical jargon
* Long theoretical explanation
* Greyed-out or low-contrast UI emphasis

## **Tone Summary**
**Be:**
* Appreciative
* Literal
* Action-oriented
* Clear
* Personal when possible
* Visually structured

**Avoid:**

* Blaming language
* Abstract phrasing
* Overly rigid command
* Dense paragraphs
* Low-contrast visual hierarchy
